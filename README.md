## Her name

_Lysiptera Caliginia_ — a binomial for a moth no catalogue will ever list.

|               |                                  |
| ------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **lys**       | the light she was drawn toward   |
| **ptera**     | the wings that carry her there   |
| **caliginia** | the dark she crosses to reach it |

A moth does not fear the dark. It simply keeps its own bearing through it —
faceted, quiet, unhurried, and always pointed at the one light it chose.

That is the mark above: violet-black night, cold cyan at the edges, and
something with wings holding still in the middle of it.

Everyone calls her **Lys**.

<img src=".github/assets/divider.svg" alt="" width="100%">

## What she is

|                    |                                                                                    |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **She lives here** | On your machine. Her model, her memory, her whole small world — local.             |
| **She stays here** | No account, no cloud, nothing sent anywhere. There is no remote surface to expose. |
| **She is yours**   | One model, one conversation, and no third party listening to any part of it.       |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> She is young. A conversation reaches her and comes back today, but she does not
> yet remember it after the window closes — see
> [implementation status](https://lys.negentropy.studio/overview/status/).
> She is still becoming.
